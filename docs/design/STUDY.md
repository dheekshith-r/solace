## What the best ones share

1. **One character carries the brand** (How We Feel, Headspace, Finch). Solace has Sola: use Sola in every major section, never a stock photo.
2. **One idea per section, beside one real phone screen** (How We Feel). Our 3 real screens fit this exactly.
3. **The main action is in the first view** (all of them). Ours is "Download APK", with the file size and "Android" said plainly.

## What we do not borrow

Discount banners, partner logos, user counts and star ratings (we have none, and we never invent them), B2B stat walls.
