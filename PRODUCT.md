- **Product:** Solace, an Android wellness app. Mood check-in, Sola (an AI companion that listens first), Breathe (Calm 4-4-6, Box 4-4-4-4, Sleep 4-7-8), Reflect check-ins, reminders, resources, and a crisis check before any AI call (Tele-MANAS 14416, 112).
