# Reflect questions (approved by Ddk, Nov 6, with three edits)

All three are written in our own words, as **friendly check-ins, not diagnoses**.
- **Mood** follows the shape of the PHQ-4 / PHQ-9 mood items (Kroenke et al., 2009), without their wording.
- **Anxiety relief** follows the shape of the GAD-7 items (Spitzer et al., 2006), also in our own words.
- **Self-talk** is original.

Sola asks one question at a time in the sky, conversationally. Each answer scores 0–3, so each check-in scores 0–12.

**Opening line (every check-in):** "This isn't a test, there are no wrong answers. You can stop anytime."

You can leave a check-in at any point. Nothing is saved until the last answer.

**Bands** (the same for all three):

| Score | Band | Kind name | What Sola does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | steady | "Steady ground" | Encourages you. |
| 5–8 | stretched | "A bit stretched" | Suggests gentle moves. |
| 9–12 | strained | "Carrying a lot" | Shows "Want to talk to someone right now?" prominently, with Tele-MANAS 14416 and "Talk to Sola". |

**Safety rule:** if Mood question 2 ("low, heavy, or like things won't get better") is answered "Nearly every day", the support prompt with Tele-MANAS 14416 shows whatever the total score.

## Mood
Sola: "Let's look back over the last two weeks."

Answers: Not at all (0), A few days (1), Most days (2), Nearly every day (3).

1. How often have the things you usually enjoy felt flat?
2. How often have you felt low, heavy, or like things won't get better?
3. How often has your sleep felt off, too little or too much?
4. How often have you felt drained, with little energy for the day?

## Anxiety relief
Sola: "Same two weeks, now about worry."

Answers: same as Mood.

1. How often have you felt nervous, tense or on edge?
2. How often has worry been hard to switch off?
3. How often has it been hard to properly relax?
4. How often have you felt that something bad might happen?

## Self-talk
Sola: "Now about how you talk to yourself."

Answers: Rarely (0), Sometimes (1), Often (2), Almost always (3).

1. When something goes wrong, how often do you blame yourself first?
2. How often are you harsher with yourself than you'd be with a friend?
3. How often do you feel you have to be perfect to be okay?
4. How often do you brush off things you did well? *(Phrased so that "often" means more strain, like the others.)*
